## Scope

`index.html`: the one-page personal portfolio at https://www.krishnanishad.com.np. Visitor mode: Experience/Persuade hybrid. The work leads, and the action is to see the projects or email.

## Audience and job

Two equal audiences:
- brand/social clients;
- dev employers and recruiters.

In one viewport they should learn that Krishna does both, then reach projects or contact. The proof is real: +447% engagement at Availclouds, the help-desk frontend, the OpenCV and UE5 projects. Nothing is invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One player, two kits. HOME (crimson/white) is the brand & social side and AWAY (navy/volt) is the developer side, with a holographic rookie card as the player's ID. The page refuses the category default, a dark dev portfolio with a particle hero.

OWN-WORLD:
- **Colour:** football-kit colourways own the ground (#C8102E/#fff or #0B1F4B/#D7FF3A, gold #C9A227 thread). The mesh weave sits on the field, and the "shorts" panel holds the record.
- **Type:** Anton drop-shadow numerals, Barlow Condensed caps labels, Barlow body.
- **Details:** a KN embroidered crest, stitched patches, a matchday-strip nav, fixture rows.

STORY: A visitor sees a crimson shirt-launch page and a foil rookie card of Krishna. They flip the kit to Away and the whole page turns navy/volt, the role types "Developer", and dev projects move first. They read the career as fixtures with results, then email.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- **Strip:** a dark matchday strip on top.
- **Left 46%:** a "Now playing: <role>" crest patch, KRISHNA / NISHAD in Anton at 64–146px with a deep drop shadow, a two-line lede, the Home/Away radio switch, then "See the work" (filled) and "Write to me".
- **Right 54%:** the WebGL rookie card in a penny sleeve. The photo is in natural colour with holo foil on its backdrop only. The card tilts to the pointer, a Flip button sits under it, and the stat sheet is on the back.

Signature interaction: the kit switch. The new kit ripples out from the pressed label (view-transition clip-path), and the card does a full turn and repaints edge-on.

FORM: Home & Away Kit, fused with the Foil Rookie Card at the user's request. Position on the ordered list: the user's pick after a 5-sample round plus 3 fusion iterations. Seed key 15c7d50a.

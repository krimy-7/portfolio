What we fix, and what we hand back.

We resolve, following your runbook

  • Password resets and MFA re-enrollment
  • Microsoft 365, Outlook and Teams problems
  • Printers, scanners and mapped drives
  • VPN and Remote Desktop access
  • RMM alerts: disk space, stopped services, failed backups, offline devices
  • Failed patches and reboots inside agreed windows
  • New-user and leaver requests from your checklist
  • Windows Server and Remote Desktop session problems

We escalate to your on-call right away

  • Suspected security incidents
  • Outages affecting a whole site or client
  • Anything that needs hands on site
  • Anything outside your runbook or approval limits

We work only inside your PSA and remote tools, under accounts you control. We never contact your clients outside your ticket system, and we never sell to them.

Questions MSPs ask us.

Where is your team?

In Kathmandu, Nepal, about 10 to 11 hours ahead of US Eastern time. Your nights are our normal working hours, so your tickets aren't handled by a tired night shift.

Will your technicians reply as my company?

Yes. We use your email signatures and ticket templates. Your clients see your brand.

Which tools can you work in?

Your PSA and RMM. Today we work in ConnectWise PSA, Autotask, HaloPSA and Syncro, and in NinjaOne, Datto RMM, N-able N-central and ConnectWise Automate. Using something else? We learn it during the pilot's shadow days.

Do you answer your clients' phone calls?

Not at launch. For now we work through tickets, email and chat.

What don't you do?

On-site visits, US daytime coverage for now, and changes outside your runbook without your approval.

Some of our clients are CPA firms. How do you handle their data?

Our technicians fix IT problems without opening tax returns or client financial files. Tell us any firm-specific rules and we'll follow them.

What's the minimum commitment?

The two-week pilot. After that, month to month with 30 days' notice.

How fast can we start?

Usually within 5 business days of our first call.

Who's behind Availclouds?

Availclouds LLC, a Nevada company. Our founders, Aarav and Sita, are listed above, and you'll meet one of them on your first call.
